# Anatolia Academic: Comprehensive Research Portal on the Republic of Turkey
## Türkiye Araştırmaları ve Akademik Portalı / Академический исследовательский портал

**Anatolia Academic** is an open, peer-reviewed, trilingual academic research portal engineered for university students, postgraduates, think-tank fellows, and international scholars researching the Republic of Turkey (*Türkiye Cumhuriyeti*) across political, constitutional, strategic, economic, demographic, and geographical dimensions.

---

## 🌐 Trilingual Architecture (English, Türkçe, Русский)

Instant one-click language switching between three comprehensive linguistic editions via the top header selector:
- **`[EN]` English:** Global academic terminology and international citation standards.
- **`[TR]` Türkçe:** Authentic Turkish legal (*anayasa hukuku, mülki idare, içtihat*), economic (*makroekonomik istikrar, katılım bankacılığı*), and historical terminology.
- **`[RU]` Русский:** Comprehensive academic Russian terminology (*Конвенция Монтрё, советско-турецкие договоры 1921 г., исполнительное президентство, Диянет, АЭС Аккую*).

All navigation labels, search indexes, module contents, citations, and metadata dynamically adapt while preserving the active reading chapter.

## 🏛️ Comprehensive Knowledge Modules (13)

### Module 01: History & Foreign Relations (Expanded 5x)
- Pre-Ottoman Anatolia, Battle of Manzikert (1071), Seljuk Sultanate of Rum, and Anatolian Beyliks.
- Classical Ottoman Empire, Millet system, Tanzimat modernization, and 1876/1908 constitutionalism.
- World War I, Gallipoli defense, Treaty of Sèvres partition, Mustafa Kemal Pasha, National Pact (*Misak-ı Millî*), War of Independence, and the 1923 Treaty of Lausanne.
- **Soviet Union & Russia Strategic Arc (1920–2026):**
  - 1920 Lenin-Atatürk solidarity, 1921 Treaty of Moscow & Treaty of Kars, 10M+ gold rubles aid.
  - 1930s Soviet technical loans (Sümerbank textile mills).
  - 1945 Stalin demands for Kars/Ardahan and joint Straits bases; Truman Doctrine & 1952 NATO accession.
  - 1962 Cuban Missile Crisis (secret removal of US Jupiter missiles from Turkey).
  - Post-Cold War "compartmentalized diplomacy": Blue Stream, TurkStream, Akkuyu Nuclear Power Plant ($20B Rosatom BOO), S-400 missile purchase, and Montreux Article 19 Straits closure in the Russia-Ukraine War.
- **Bulgarian & Balkan Axis (1913–2026):**
  - 1913 Treaty of Constantinople, 1925 Ankara Treaty of Friendship.
  - Cold War Thracian frontier (NATO vs. Warsaw Pact).
  - The 1989 *Büyük Göç*: Zhivkov regime's forced assimilation campaigns (*Văzroditelen protses*) and the mass exodus of over 360,000 Bulgarian Turks into Turkey.
  - Post-1989 democratization, the DPS party, Kapıkule border crossing (2nd busiest customs gate in the world), and BOTAŞ-Bulgargaz gas interconnectors.
- **Regional & Multilateral:** Greece (Aegean, Cyprus), Syria & Iraq (GAP transboundary waters), Iran (1639 Kasr-ı Şirin), South Caucasus (2021 Shusha Declaration), NATO, and EU accession.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Diplomatic timeline (1918–2026), Sovereign Treaties Matrix, Soviet-Bulgarian Geopolitical Indices.

### Module 02: Geography, Topography & Earth Systems

- Fauna, national parks, water stress (1,350 m³/capita), and 2053 net-zero carbon goals.

### Module 04: Constitutional Law & Evolution (Expanded 5x)
- Pre-republican constitutionalism: Sened-i İttifak (1808), Kanûn-ı Esâsî (1876), 1909 reforms.
- 1921 Teşkilât-ı Esâsîye (Assembly government) & 1924 Republican Constitution (progressive secularization).
- 1961 Constitution (Constitutional Court creation, bicameralism, civil liberties) vs. 1982 Constitution (post-coup military charter, Kenan Evren regime, state security bias, 10% threshold).
- Entrenched Unamendable Core: Articles 1, 2, 3, protected by Article 4.
- Comprehensive 2017 Constitutional Overhaul: Executive Presidency (*Cumhurbaşkanlığı Hükümet Sistemi*), abolition of Prime Ministry, presidential decree powers (*Cumhurbaşkanlığı Kararnamesi*), partisan presidency.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Comparative Constitutional Matrix (1876 vs 1921 vs 1924 vs 1961 vs 1982 vs 2017), Benchmarks Stat Grid, Constitutional Turning Points Timeline.

### Module 05: State Architecture & Government System (Expanded 5x)
- The Executive Presidency, 9 Presidential Policy Councils, autonomous agencies (MİT, SSB, SBB, Diyanet, İletişim).
- 17 line ministries, Presidential Cabinet meetings, ministerial non-parliamentary status.
- Grand National Assembly (TBMM): 600 seats, D'Hondt proportional representation, 7% threshold, statutory lawmaking, budget approvals, and oversight tools.
- Field Administration: 81 provincial governors (*Vali*), 973 district governors (*Kaymakam*), and 30 Metropolitan Municipalities (*Büyükşehir Belediyeleri*).
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Full State Institutional Organogram, Central vs Local Governance Matrix, Administrative Benchmarks Grid.

### Module 06: Armed Forces & Civil-Military Dynamics (Expanded 5x)
- Turkish Armed Forces (TSK): Land, Naval (*Mavi Vatan*), and Air branches; NATO LANDCOM & Incirlik base.
- Coup Chronicles: 1960 Coup, 1971 Memorandum, 1980 Coup, 1997 Post-Modern Coup, 2007 E-Memorandum.
- July 15, 2016 Attempted Coup and historic post-2016 civilianization: General Staff subordinated to the Ministry of National Defense (MSB), civilianized YAŞ, National Defense University (MSÜ), Gendarmerie transfer to Interior.
- Indigenous Defense Industry: SSB, ASELSAN, TUSAŞ (KAAN 5th gen stealth jet), Baykar (Bayraktar TB2/Akıncı/Kızılelma), ROKETSAN, >80% domestic ratio, $5.5B+ annual exports.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Military Interventions Matrix, Civil-Military Command Organogram, Defense Industrial Benchmarks Grid.

### Module 07: Judicial System & Rule of Law (Expanded 5x)
- Civil law heritage; bifurcated ordinary (civil/criminal) vs. administrative jurisdictions.
- Three-tiered court system: First Instance, BAM/BİM Regional Courts of Appeal, Court of Cassation (*Yargıtay*), and Council of State (*Danıştay*).
- Constitutional Court (*Anayasa Mahkemesi - AYM*): 15 jurists, norm control, party closure, and the Individual Application (*Bireysel Başvuru*) human rights mechanism (75,000+ cases/yr).
- Council of Judges and Prosecutors (HSK), UYAP national digital court network, and Article 90 ECHR supremacy.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Bifurcated Court Hierarchy Organogram, High Judicial Bodies Matrix, Judicial Metrics Grid.

### Module 08: Demography & Ethnic Tapestry (Expanded 5x)
- Population statistics (85.37M), hyper-urbanization (93.2%), fertility decline (1.51), and aging society.
- Sociolinguistic mosaic: Turks, Kurds (15–20%), Zazas, Arabs, Circassians (*Adyghe*), Laz, Bosniaks, Romani.
- Treaty of Lausanne non-Muslim confessional minorities: Armenians, Sephardic Jews, Greeks (*Rûm*), and ancient Syriacs.
- Modern migration: 3.1M+ Syrians under Temporary Protection, 2016 EU-Turkey Statement.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Sociolinguistic & Confessional Mosaic Matrix, Demographic Metrics Grid, Migration Waves Timeline.

### Module 09: Transportation & Logistics Corridors (Expanded 5x)
- High-Speed Rail (TCDD YHT): Ankara–Istanbul, Konya, Sivas, Halkalı–Kapıkule, and the Marmaray subsea rail tunnel.
- Highway engineering megaprojects: 1915 Çanakkale Bridge (world's longest suspension span: 2,023m), Yavuz Sultan Selim Bridge, Osmangazi Bridge, Eurasia Tunnel.
- Maritime Straits transit: 40,000+ vessel passages/year under the 1936 Montreux Convention, VTS vessel traffic radar systems, commercial ports of Ambarlı and Mersin.
- Global aviation crossroads: Istanbul Airport (IGA, 76M+ passengers), Turkish Airlines (THY, 129 countries).
- Trans-Caspian International Transport Route (Middle Corridor / *Orta Koridor*) and Baku–Tbilisi–Kars railway.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Engineering Megaprojects Matrix, Transport Network Benchmarks Grid, Modern Infrastructure Milestones Timeline.

### Module 10: Energy Resources, Consumption & Regulation (Expanded 5x)
- Primary energy balance: ~70% import dependency, 50–55 bcm natural gas consumption.
- Domestic upstream breakthrough: Sakarya deepwater Black Sea gas field (710 bcm reserve).
- Strategic pipelines: TANAP (Southern Gas Corridor), TurkStream, Blue Stream, Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan (BTC).
- Nuclear power: Akkuyu NPP (4,800 MW, 4x VVER-1200 under Rosatom Build-Own-Operate model).
- Renewable electricity: 107,000+ MW capacity, 54% renewables (hydro, solar YEKA, wind, world #4 in geothermal).
- Regulatory bodies: Energy Market Regulatory Authority (EPDK), BOTAŞ, EÜAŞ, TEİAŞ.
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Strategic Hydrocarbon Pipelines Matrix, National Energy Balance Grid, Energy Megaprojects Timeline.

### Module 11: National Economy & Regulatory Institutions (Expanded 5x)
- Macroeconomic profile: $1.1+ Trillion nominal GDP, services (55%), industry (28%), agriculture (6%).
- Manufacturing export powerhouse: $255B+ annual exports, automotive assembly leader (Ford Otosan, Renault, Tofaş, Toyota, Togg), electronics (Arçelik, Vestel), textiles, steel.
- 1995 EU-Turkey Customs Union and international trade partners (Germany, USA, Russia, China).
- Regulatory institutions: Competition Authority (*Rekabet Kurumu*), Public Procurement Authority (KİK), Treasury & Finance, SBB.

### Module 12: Financial System & Banking Architecture (Expanded 5x)
- Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey (TCMB): Price stability mandate, Monetary Policy Committee (PPK), 1-week repo rate, FX reserve management.
- Post-2001 banking restructuring: Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BDDK), Basel III capital adequacy (>17%), Capital Markets Board (SPK), TMSF deposit insurance.
- Commercial banking structure: State banks (Ziraat, Halk, Vakıf), private commercial banks (İş Bankası, Garanti BBVA, Akbank), participation (Islamic) banking (~9% assets).
- Capital markets: Borsa Istanbul (BIST 100/30) and the Turkey Wealth Fund (TVF).
- *Visuals & Schemes:* Financial Institutions Matrix, Financial Capital Benchmarks Grid, Modern Financial History Timeline.

### Module 13: Religion & Religious Institutions (Expanded 5x)
- Constitutional secularism (*Laiklik*): French *laïcité* heritage, active state regulation under Article 2.
- Presidency of Religious Affairs (*Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı*): Law No. 429, 140,000+ staff, 90,000+ mosques, unified Friday sermons, fatwas.

- Alevi-Bektashi tradition: Cemevi worship, semah sacred dance, 2022 Alevi-Bektashi Directorate.

- *Visuals & Schemes:* Religious Taxonomy Matrix, Diyanet & Demographic Metrics Grid, Secularization Policy Timeline.
